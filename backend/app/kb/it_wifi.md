# Office WiFi Troubleshooting

If your company laptop cannot connect to the office WiFi, first verify that WiFi is enabled and airplane mode is turned off. Disconnect from the office network and reconnect using your company credentials.

If the connection still fails, restart the laptop and try again. You can also forget the saved WiFi network and reconnect.

If other employees are also unable to connect, the issue may be related to the office network rather than the individual device.

If these steps do not resolve the problem, contact the IT support team and provide the device name and a description of the error.
