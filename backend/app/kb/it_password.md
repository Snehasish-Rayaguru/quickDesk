# Company Password Guidelines

Employees should use strong and unique passwords for company accounts. Passwords should not be shared with colleagues, support staff, or other people.

If you believe your company password has been compromised, change it immediately through the approved account management system.

Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthday, phone number, or common words.

If you are unable to change your password or cannot access your account, contact IT support for assistance.
