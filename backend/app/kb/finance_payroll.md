# Payroll Information

Employee salaries are processed according to the company's payroll schedule.

Employees should review their salary information and payslips through the approved payroll system.

If there is an unexpected difference in salary, deduction, or other payroll information, contact the Finance or Payroll team.

When reporting a payroll issue, provide the relevant pay period and a description of the discrepancy. Do not send passwords or other sensitive account credentials.
